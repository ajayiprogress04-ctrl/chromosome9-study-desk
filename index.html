Quick guide

  1. Choose an objective. One example and one question appear. Submit your answer, review feedback and Continue to the next example.
  2. Tap an underlined term. Returning restores the same lesson and reading position.
  3. Use flashcards for recall, then compare your answer before rating it.
  4. The example comes before its immediate question. Hints help before submission. Use the rubric dropdown to assess a written response.
  5. Use Practice for the existing question bank and Presentation for oral rehearsal.
  6. Save notes and export a backup. Browser storage does not synchronize devices.

The manuscript is the scientific authority. These learning objectives are inferred from its sections.
