# Change notes

## 1.0.0

Created the complete local learning project from the supplied manuscript, oral presentation, poster and App_Preferences.md. Added structured science and a compact three-theme interface in the reference design family. No reference-app files or source presentations were modified. No network service is needed for the core app. Static and simulated-DOM tests are included, with remaining browser checks stated explicitly.

Significant files: index.html; styles.css; app.js; core.js; content/research.json; content/source-extract.json; content/content.js; assets/; sources/; tests/; scripts/.

## 1.1.0

Deepened all three theme palettes; expanded glossary to 102 source-context entries with aliases; linked and underlined scientific terminology throughout readable content and glossary explanations; added figure-specific glossary keys; reorganised all 15 lessons under six numbered learning objectives in two expandable classes. Existing read markers, notes, bookmarks, answers and card schedules keep their ids and storage key. Updated App_Preferences to version 4. Original source documents and image pixels are unchanged.

Verify the actual deployed URL and platform previews after deployment; cached or protected pages may not display a card.
